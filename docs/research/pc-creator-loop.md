# PC Creator: the job/order loop

Only the job loop is borrowed from PC Creator (brief). Accessed 2026-09-30.

## Publisher note

The brief says "PC Creator (SeStudio Publishing)". What I found:
- The PC Creator fandom wiki says PC Creator is "by UltraAndre", on Android and iOS.
- The App Store lists PC Creator 2's seller as **CREATY GLOBAL (Creaty Global OU)**, and its Play Store id is `com.ultraandre.pccreator2`.
- **I found no page connecting "SeStudio" to PC Creator.** It may be a different listing or a regional publisher. Worth confirming with the user.

## How orders work (sourced)

**PC Creator 1**, from the fandom wiki "Customer orders" page, read through the MediaWiki API: https://pc-creator.fandom.com/wiki/Customer_orders

| Order type | How it's completed | Average pay (wiki) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Optimize OS | "Download and install the software that the customer want" | $247 |
| Upgrade computer | "Upgrade the customer's PC to meet the customer's requirements" | $392 |
| Create new PC | "Create a PC that meets the customer's requirements" | $1,916 |
| Optimize FPS | "Upgrade the customer's PC parts to meet the given FPS requirements" | $439 |
| Create Quantum PC | At level 30, build from quantum items. "Does not pay, gives a trophy instead and is the technical 'end'" | trophy |

- **Rooms** in PC Creator 1 cap how many PCs you hold at once: garage 2, apartment 3, office 5, pro room 7, pro room V2 8, pro room V3 12. Source: https://pc-creator.fandom.com/wiki/Rooms
- **In-game money** is "approximately 1:1 to the US dollar". Source: https://pc-creator.fandom.com/wiki/Money

**PC Creator 2:**
- The developer's App Store text says: "Take on challenging customer requests, each with their own unique requirements and budgets. Successfully completing these orders earns you money, strengthens your reputation, and opens up even more opportunities to expand your business." It also says money and experience come from completing client tasks.
  - Source: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/pc-creator-2-computer-tycoon/id1604170642 (page opened)
- A third-party beginner's guide says:
  - "Tap on the chat icon to get orders from clients, chat with them and tell them the prices according to the work they need."
  - "When a customer gives you an order, you have the option to set the price... it doesn't affect anything like the relationship with the customer so far we have seen in the game."
  - "Buy parts or software and do the work to complete the order and send the client's PC back to them to collect the rewards."
  - Source: https://mobilegaminghub.com/pc-creator-2-computer-tycoon-beginners-guide-to-get-started/ (page opened)
- The PC Creator 2 wiki lists orders, quests, daily tasks, a season pass, a jackpot minigame, crypto exchange, selling items and PCs, ads, and gems as ways to get money. Source: https://pc-creator-2.fandom.com/wiki/Money

## What maps onto Five Nines

These points are the loop shape the brief already describes, not new mechanics:
- The client asks through a request card that has requirements and a budget.
- The player buys parts from menus, does the work, and hands the build back.
- The payout is money + xp. PC Creator 2 also has "reputation", **which the brief does not include. Not adopted unless the user asks.**
- In PC Creator 2 the player sets the price. **The brief doesn't mention this, so it's not adopted.**
